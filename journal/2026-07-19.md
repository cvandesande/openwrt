### 2026-07-19, tenth session — CI image flash, RFC 4638, SELinux discovery

Flashed the CI-built `25.12.5-mono1` image (`mono-ask-v25.12.5-r6`, rev
`r33051-f5dae5ece4`). Post-flash: journal active, `eth4` `maxmtu` 1500→1578,
UCI config survived byte-for-byte, `wan10g` reconnected on its own, cmm under
procd with `respawn 10 5 0` intact.

**RFC 4638 baby-jumbo PPPoE proven — two-part gotcha.** Setting only the VLAN
child's MTU silently did nothing: **a VLAN child cannot exceed its parent's
*live* MTU**, and bare `eth4` had no UCI `device` stanza at all. Fix: an
explicit `config device` for `eth4` at `mtu=1508` alongside the child's. With
both at a genuine 1508 pppd negotiated `Max-Payload: 1500`; SIRO's BRAS really
does RFC 4638, error counters zero. Detail in the `sdk-dpaa-mtu-hard-cap`
memory.

`pppoe-wan10g` now has a real kernel `mtu 1500` (was 1492). This does NOT
change the internet-path MTU — a LAN client's max unfragmented ping payload
stays 1472, normal for any connection (BusyBox `ping -D` is timestamping, not
don't-fragment; that's `-M do`, unavailable there).

**QoS shaper migrated `eth0`→`eth4`.** The `cmm` QM/shaper binds to a
**physical port name** (not the PPP interface), the rate unit is **Kbps**, and
it is **egress/TX-only**. It counts full L2 frame bytes while speedtests
measure TCP goodput — a consistent **~6-7% gap** that is expected, not a bug.
Tuned to `rate 107000 bucketsize 8192`. Full facts in `cmm-qm-shaper-facts`.

**SELinux discovered: WireGuard was never config-broken.** LuCI showed "no
devices configured" with byte-identical UCI config. Real cause: the policy
blocked `wg syncconf` from netifd — `wireguard.wg.subj` had everything except
`open` on `netif.server.subj` fifo_file, and `wg` re-opens its inherited stdin
pipe via `/proc/self/fd/0`. Initial-sync-time block only. Fixed by patch 011.
The post-flash sweep then produced patch 012 (PKG_RELEASE 3): confined
`xtables` domain, rpcd→wireguard.wg transition, scoped mwan3 runtmp reads for
rpcd, hotplug lock/conf grants, libtmp↔xattr.fs for podman. See the
`selinux-wireguard-syncconf-denied` memory and
`docs/06-selinux-audit-diagnostics.md`.

Also this session: haproxy.cfg was found reverted to the stock example and
restored by hand from a backup. The theory that sysupgrade had dropped it was
**disproven 2026-07-26** — see the closed #27 above; the backup/restore/reinstall
chain all works, and this loss remains unexplained. The CDX classifier desync
from repeated `network reload` was first seen here — now #25.
