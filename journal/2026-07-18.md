**Fast-path proof methodology (sessions 2–3)**: DAC loopback (eth3↔eth4 wired
to each other) **cannot** prove fast-path — cmm's model is exactly one ingress
+ one egress port per flow; a forced hairpin is two forwarding decisions
collapsed into one conntrack entry whose endpoints are the veths, structurally
invisible to cmm. Also: cmm never shows ICMP at all (use TCP/UDP), and
short-lived flows never promote — you need a sustained large transfer to see
`fp-state: installed`. Real proof shape: a real third-party host on the 10G
port, single-hop routed flow, e.g. `br-mz`/`eth2` in → `eth4` out. Cross-zone
forwarding needs an explicit `config forwarding` pair; zone `forward ACCEPT` is
intra-zone only.

**Offload proven (sessions 3, 6)**: bidirectional hardware offload
(`fp-state: installed`, `local-conn: no`, `fpp-dir: orig+reply`) proven on 6.12
and re-proven on 6.18, for IPv4 AND IPv6 (fast-path is address-family-agnostic;
IPv6-over-PPPoE-WAN specifically untested). Corroboration method on 6.18:
netdev packet counts orders of magnitude below the transferred volume (the

### 2026-07-18, eighth session — both PPPoE-over-10G root causes

**Root cause 1 — periodic PPPoE drops: 10GBASE-T over a Cat5e patch lead.** The
breakthrough was direct register access to the SFP module's internal PHY (FS
SFP-10GM-T-30, no DDM, Marvell Alaska X): it answers the **Rollball
MDIO-over-I2C protocol** via `i2csfp /dev/i2c-9 rollball read <devad> <reg>`
(bus 8 = eth3 cage, bus 9 = eth4 cage). Key registers: `1.1` PMA status (bit 2 =
copper link), `3.0x8008` Marvell copper status, `7.32`/`7.33`
advertisement/partner, write `7.0=0x3200` to restart autoneg. **Under
`fixed-link` the kernel's `carrier` is always 1** — these registers are the only
copper-link visibility. Reference: `docs/05-sfp-module-diagnostics.md` and the
`sfp-module-phy-rollball-access` memory.

The copper link to the ONT trained at 10G and retrained ~4x/minute on an idle
link; each retrain is a multi-second outage → 5 lost LCP echoes → `Modem
hangup`, with ~7% FCS errors from the same marginality. The ONT patch lead was
**Cat5e, not rated for 10GBASE-T** (an earlier desktop test ran clean because
that peer was a 5G-max NIC). Capping the module's advert to 5G gave a
113-minute zero-drop session over Cat5e; after fitting **Cat6**, full 10G
trained with zero drops and zero errors in 7.1M frames. Nothing was defective —
the cable was the single variable. `i2c-tools`/`i2csfp` added to the seed
(`88ba64a19e`); a re-cap helper survives at `/etc/sfp10g-limit-rate`
(router-only, hooks removed).

**Root cause 2 — silent fast-path death: cmm use-after-free + no supervision.**
cmm had SIGSEGV'd at boot and nothing restarted it (the old init only retried
initial startup) — the CPU path kept working while the hardware fast path was
silently dead. Real UAF in ask-cmm `route_cache.c`: `__cmmCtRouteUpdate()` and
`__cmmCtTunnelRouteUpdate()` read `route->flags` *after* `__cmmRouteDeregister()`
can free the route — a guaranteed freed-memory read on every route invalidation
for the last referencing conntrack. The flapping 10G link produced route churn
in bulk, so the two bugs were one story. PC resolved via addr2line against the
unstripped build. Fixes: `ask-cmm@0362f33` (hoist the flags read),
`ask-cmm@3e52378` (new `cmm -F` foreground mode), openwrt `c647dabe92` (pin
bump, PKG_RELEASE 25, **procd conversion with `respawn 10 5 0`**). Respawn
proven live (`kill -SEGV` → respawned → 73 flows re-promoted). Offload proven:
WAN flows `fp-state: installed` with `orig-oif: pppoe-wan10g` both directions;
no FMC/PCD changes needed (`cdx_pcd.xml` already routes `cdx_pppoe_dist` on
every port).
